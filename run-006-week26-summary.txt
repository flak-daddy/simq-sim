2. THE MARKET: A TAIL SEED
--------------------------
Regimes over the 26 weeks: calm_up 12, chop 6, rebound 5, crash 2, selloff 1.
SimQ went from $63.80 to $266.22 (+317%). Weeks 1-13 were mostly flat to firm ($58-$84);
W14-W19 ran it from $83.57 to $179.58 (chop +10.5%/+7.1%, two rebounds +16.9%/+16.3%, calm_up
+19.3%, rebound +12.0%). W22-W23 were two crash weeks that barely dented it (-2.6%, -1.5%), then
W24 rebound +8.1%, a W25 "selloff" week that closed +26.0% (three big up days; a +2.3 standard
deviation draw for that regime) and a W26 rebound +15.4%.

How rare: in 20,000 random seeds on the same model (market v3), the median SimQ at Week 26 is
$66, the 99th percentile $204 and the 99.9th $290. Only 0.16% of seeds (about 1 in 600) reach
$266 or more. Checked on Oct 28 (Week 17 scan and again here): the engine replays correctly;
this is luck in the draws, not a bug. Three of the five rebound weeks (W16, W17, W19) had no
selloff or crash in the 3 weeks before; W24 and W26 followed the W22-23 crashes and the W25
selloff. That pattern led to the opt-in market v4 rebound rule (claude/market-v4-rebound-rule.txt),
which would have blocked W16, W17 and W19.
Note the W26 Thursday and Friday closes are both $266.22: Friday moved +0.002%, which rounds to
the same cent. Not a bug.
