3. YOUR BEHAVIOR: PATTERNS IN THE JOURNAL
------------------------------------------
- The midweek roll is your core routine. Close the Monday position once most
  of the premium is gone, then sell a closer strike for Thursday and Friday.
  The median midweek put sat about 9% below spot in the second half, versus
  about 16% for Monday puts.

- You now close losing puts at midweek instead of holding them. In Run 1 you
  held both deep in-the-money puts into assignment. In Run 2's second half you
  closed every losing position at Wednesday and rolled it lower. Two of those
  rolls (W31, W35) were still assigned; two (W41, W45) expired.

- Covered calls at or above cost. On lots 2 and 4 every call was above the
  purchase price. On lot 3 you rolled C70 up to C74 after the rebound, paying
  $280 to avoid selling the shares $1 below cost. All four lots closed at a
  stock profit. This fixes the "calls below cost" pattern from Run 1.

- Two-put Mondays. From W42 you split cash across two strikes (e.g. 50 and 40,
  49 and 40, 56 and 57). Every one of those weeks ended up.

- You waited more selectively. Monday passes in W40 and W50 were both followed
  by a midweek put that expired.

5. WEAKNESSES
-------------
- Roll-downs in falling weeks can still end in assignment. W31 and W35 both
  rolled to a strike that SimQ closed below on Friday. Paying to close and
  then being assigned anyway costs twice. It worked out here because the
  shares were called away at a profit within 2-3 weeks.

- Selloff-week Monday cushions. The three second-half losing puts (W35 P67,
  W41 P57, W45 P65) were sold 9-13% below spot in selloff weeks. Selloff
  weeks with wide cushions (W52 at 22-23%) were fine. One week's move in a 3x
  product can exceed 13% (W45 fell 24%).

- Late-week premium is thin. Many midweek rolls collected $8-$20 for the last
  two days (W42 P59 $20, W46 P51.5 $10, W47 P57 $10, W52 P60 $8), while tying
  up $5,000-$6,000 of collateral for that time.

- Full-cash Mondays leave no room to act. With two puts sold, free cash was
  often under $1,000 at Wednesday. You could only add a position by closing
  one first.


6. THINGS TO TRY IN RUN 3 (process, not strike picks)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Before a midweek roll in a falling week, decide in advance whether you would
  accept assignment on the new strike. If not, consider closing without rolling.
- Write down your Monday cushion for each regime type and check it against
  results at Week 26.
- Track how much of each week's profit comes from the Thursday-Friday roll
  versus the Monday trade, to see whether the roll is worth the collateral.
- Keep the covered-call rule (calls at or above cost). It worked on every lot.
- Keep comparing against the cash benchmark, which is about +2% per 26 weeks.
